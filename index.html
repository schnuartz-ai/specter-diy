Specter-DIY ESP32-P4 Flasher

Experimental builds of an unofficial port, flashed from the browser

⚠ Your browser does not support the Web Serial API. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave on a desktop computer.

⚠ Unofficial port, not official Specter-DIY

This flasher writes firmware from sandman21vs/specter-diy, an unofficial fork that runs Specter-DIY on the Waveshare ESP32-P4 4.3" board.

  • Do not use it with real seeds or real funds. Testnet and throwaway keys only.
  • Not reviewed or endorsed by the Specter-DIY maintainers, never audited, and tested on one board.
  • Secure boot and flash encryption are not enabled: anyone with physical access to the board can read or replace what is on it.
  • Flashing wipes everything stored on the device. Builds are development snapshots and may misbehave.
  • No warranty and no support. Report problems to this fork, not to upstream Specter-DIY.

Want the real thing? Use the official Specter-DIY on its supported hardware.

How to flash
  1. Plug the board into its UART USB-C port with a data cable.
  2. Pick the firmware below (the latest release is selected).
  3. Click Connect and choose the board's UART serial port.
  4. If the error says Failed to open serial port, close Kern, serial monitors and other browser tabs using the port, then retry. BOOT/RESET only helps when the chip fails to sync.
  5. Flash only the exact board and chip revisions listed above; the page blocks unsupported ESP32-P4 revisions.
  6. After flashing, the board resets and starts Specter.

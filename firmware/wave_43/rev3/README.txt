Sandman ESP32-P4 v3.x community build for Waveshare 4.3-C
Based on Sandman esp32p4-v0.1.0-alpha.7
Compiled for ESP32-P4 silicon revisions v3.0-v3.99.
This experimental build has not been tested on the recipient's physical board.
Flashing writes storage-reset.bin at 0x410000 and erases internal device storage.
Do not use with real seeds or funds.
